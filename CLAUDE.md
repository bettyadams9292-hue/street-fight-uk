# Street Fight UK: notes for Claude

Read this first. Two people work on this repo, each with their own Claude: Leeko (the owner) and his friend. Keep this file up to date: add a line to the work log whenever a PR is merged.

## How we work

- **Branches and PRs.** Never commit to `dev` or `main` directly. Make a branch, push it, and open a PR into `dev`. Vercel deploys `dev` to https://street-fight-uk.vercel.app on every merge.
- **Merging.** Merge only when the person you're working for says "merge". Don't merge on your own initiative.
- **Visual regression CI.** It fails on `dev` because the saved screenshots are out of date, not because of a code fault. The owner refreshes them in GitHub: Actions → CI → Run workflow → `update_baselines`. "Type check, tests, build" is the check that matters.
- **Checks before a PR.** Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`. Check any UI change at both phone landscape (844×390) and phone portrait (390×700).
- **Credits.** Third-party CC BY assets must be credited in the folder's CREDITS/SOURCES file. Art the owner made or supplied needs no licence. The owner has chosen to keep the hydroponics pack in the public repo even though its licence says not to redistribute it.
- **Credit the creator.** Projects are credited to Leeko.

## Where things live

**Asset sets** (`src/render/props/`). Each set loads GLBs from `public/models/<set>/`:
- `GymLibrary`, `ShopLibrary`, `HydroLibrary`, `SecurityLibrary`, `DoorLibrary`, `KenneyLibrary` (furniture and food), and `PropLibrary` (weapons and small items).
- Shared loader: `modelSet.ts`. Some sets are used in `LevelView.ts` through the helpers `fit()` (Kenney), `fitting()` (shop), `gym()` and `piece()` (Polyfork kit).

**Streets** (`src/city/`). Built from the city kit (`public/models/city`).
- `street.ts`: `PROP_MODELS` lists the street props the kit draws. `KIT_KINDS` in `catalogue.ts` lists the building fronts it draws.
- Anything not covered there is built from boxes in `LevelView.ts`.

**Doors** (`src/city/doorSpots.ts`). Doors are random styles from DoorLibrary. In `World.findInteractable`, an exit is a strip in front of the door, so you use it standing at the door.
- Blue door boards show only over points of interest: `isPoiExit` at the end of `LevelView.ts` (shops, gyms, pub, casino, routes the story opens).

**Characters** (`src/characters/human/`, `public/models/human`).
- MakeHuman-style body plus clothing and hair proxies. `fromAppearance.ts` maps the creator's choices to models.
- Clothes with no proxy (hoodies, jackets, jeans and so on) are made in code by `garments.ts`.

**Animation.**
- Mocap: `public/anim/rokoko_mocap.json` (fight clips), `mixamo_mocap.json` (the owner's Mixamo library, `tools/mixamo/convert.py`) and `ara_mocap.json` (walk, run, sit and so on).
- Everything else is keyed in code: `src/render/poses.ts`, `src/render/activityPoses.ts`.

**UI** (`src/ui/`).
- `styles.css` is the base. `theme.css` is the "Council Estate Grime" design system: a red, white and blue palette, Big Shoulders and Overpass fonts. It loads after `styles.css`, so put new styling in `theme.css`.
- Portrait phone layout: the `@media (orientation: portrait) and (max-width: 700px)` blocks at the end of `theme.css`.
- The in-game phone shows Me, Crew and Settings inside itself. `ui/phone/embedCss.ts` judges every size `@media` rule against an upright phone (390×700), not the real screen, so those screens look the same on an iPhone, an iPad and a desktop. Nothing to do when you add a media rule; `docs/PHONE.md` has the details, the app list and a by-hand checklist. `npm run phone` (after a build) works every phone app at eight screen sizes.

**Painted titles** (`public/art/titles/*.webp`).
- `src/ui/titles.ts`: `TITLE_SLUGS`, `titleArt()`, `titleFor()`.
- `menuButton(label, …, art)` in `controls.ts` shows the graphic in place of the text.
- New buttons need a graphic. Generate it with `tools/titles/gen.py` (instructions are in the file), then add the slug to `TITLE_SLUGS`. `tests/titles.test.ts` fails if the list and the folder disagree.

**Logo and icons.** `public/art/logo*.webp`, `menu-keyart*.webp`, `public/icons/`, `public/manifest.webmanifest`.

**Sound.** `src/audio/AudioManager.ts` uses the recorded mp3s in `public/audio`. Menu sounds, ambience and household sounds are synthesised in code. Music: `AudioManager.setMusic('menu' | 'street' | 'boss' | null)`, chosen each frame by `Game.updateMusic` (no music in rooms listed in `INDOOR_THEMES`). The title screen plays Leeko's "Hard Graft Pays Off" and the streets "Backstreet Mood" (`music_menu.mp3`, `music_street.mp3`, made into seamless loops by `tools/audio/loop_music.py`; the loop points are `MUSIC_LOOPS` in `AudioManager.ts`). Don't re-run `tools/audio/make_music.py` over them: it only makes the boss loop now.

**Materials and textures.** The owner's texture library (130 ambientCG sets) is in `public/textures/lib`.
- `src/render/materials/library.ts` loads sets on demand and makes the materials; `assign.ts` decides what gets which; `streetDetail.ts` lays asphalt, concrete and brick on the kit streets.
- `docs/assets/TEXTURE_CATALOGUE.md` lists every set; `docs/assets/TEXTURE_ASSIGNMENTS.md` says where each is used and which are still spare.
- The owner wants realistic surfaces: use a library set for new surfaces rather than a flat colour or a drawn texture.

**Tutorial.** `src/tutorial/`, design and coverage matrix in `docs/TUTORIAL.md`.
- `lessons.ts`: the chapters (run in order on a new game) and the one-off hints, as data. Each step has its words, keys, the HUD elements it lights, and the real event or state that completes it.
- `TutorialManager.ts`: listens to the game's own events, runs Mo's lessons (`World.startLesson`, `ai/TutorialBrain.ts`), and keeps its place in `save.tutorial`. Old saves count as done.
- `controls.ts`: prompts read from `KEY_BINDINGS`, `MENU_KEYS`, `PAD_BUTTONS` and `PAD_EXTRA`, so they can't name a key the game doesn't use.
- `overlay.ts`: the card in the HUD column, and the rings over the HUD.
- `library.ts`: Pause → Tutorials.
- `GUIDES`: lessons for things you meet later (06 Jobs, 07 The hotbar, 08 Dealing), each started the first time you meet its thing once the first-run lessons are done (`start`; `TutorialManager.startGuide`), kept in `save.tutorial.guide`, and in Pause → Tutorials to start or replay. `GUIDE_CHAPTERS` in `state.ts` must match.
- A new feature should add a hint to `HINTS`, a guide to `GUIDES` (for anything with a few steps to learn), or a step to a chapter.

**Hotbar.** `src/items/hotbar.ts`: four slots (1 medical, 2 food, 3 drink, 4 weapon) in `save.hotbar` by item id; a slot uses the next of its item and, when that runs out, pulls in the next suitable thing in your pockets (`hotbarStack`, `refillHotbar`). `World.useHotbar` applies it, in a fight too, with `HOTBAR_COOLDOWN` between heals (weapons never wait); `Game.updateHotbar` takes presses (1–4, Back + X / Y / A / B on a pad, the HUD slots) and holds one briefly while you're reeling. Changed in Pockets & bag (`inventoryUI.ts`). `tests/hotbar.test.ts`.

**Jobs.** `src/jobs/`: missions and work from NPCs.
- `contacts.ts`: the five contacts (Terry Walsh in the Red Lion, Bev Okafor outside the High Street bookies, Kez by the Hollins garages, Nails and Dodgy Del in the Back Alleys; Del and Nails open after Sully and the Gaffer), their kinds of work, their rewards, and each one's three chain jobs (`CHAINS`). `contactStation` puts them in a level (station `contact`, `tab` their id), clear of the street's fights and of the walking lanes (`tests/npc.test.ts`; the narrow market had no room).
- `jobs.ts`: boards (two jobs a contact, redrawn every `BOARD_TRIPS`), chain jobs (open with standing), passer-by jobs (`streetJob`), pay (`jobPay`), heat (`jobHeat`, added with `addHeatOf` where it happened), `acceptJob` / `completeJob` / `failJob` / `checkJob` (too slow, or the parcel, package or goods gone). `save.ts` is the save shape (`save.jobs`: standing, boards, chains, the one active job, contacts met).
- Kinds: sort someone out, collect a debt (pays or fights), deliver a parcel (timed, paid on the doorstep), get something back (dropped when he's down), buy and sell (Kez's package, `job_package`, stolen so the police take it; a sale logs `dealing` on your record; sometimes a rip-off fight). Job items are `jobItem` quest items.
- `World`: `setUpJob` puts the target and mates (or the recipient, debtor, buyer) at the job's spot, standing neutral till you're close or say the wrong thing (`jobSquareUp`); `setUpStreetGiver` sometimes stands a passer-by with a job on a street (`STREET_CHANCE`, not during the tutorial); USE on them is `kind: 'job'`; `job:offer`, `job:step`, `job:done`, `job:failed` events. The objective plate shows the job (with its clock) while you're on one.
- UI: `ui/jobsUI.ts` (contact screen, passer-by offer, job cards); the phone's Jobs app (the job, Directions, Drop the job, every contact); map pins; `Hud.updateJobMarks` (yellow ! over someone with a job, red over your target); GraftWiki's Jobs article; the 06 Jobs tutorial guide. Going down with something to lose fails the job (`Game`, `player:defeated`). `tests/jobs.test.ts`.

**Clothes and jewellery bonuses.** `src/items/gearBonus.ts`.
- Clothes come in five tiers, their rarity: Basic, Branded, Designer, Luxury, Iconic (`TIER_NAMES`). Each style has a line of stats (`TOP_LINES`, `BOTTOM_LINES`, `SHOE_LINES`, `ACC_LINES`) scaled by tier, set on the item when it's defined (`clothingStats`, called by the clothing helpers in `items.ts`); fight kit (wraps, gumshield, head guard) and the lucky chain keep their own. Threads sells every tier, up to the Iconic pieces.
- Outfit buffs (`outfitBuffs`): a whole fit (top, bottoms, shoes) by its lowest tier (Fresh, Dressed to kill, Dripping, Iconic fit), and matching sets (Full tracky, On site, Smart casual, Training kit).
- Jewellery (`jewelBonus`): XP by rarity and metal (a diamond adds more), and a buff by kind (rings damage, watches stamina back, necklaces poise, bracelets attack speed, earrings Technique, grills less damage taken) and by stone; four or six pieces on is drip (more XP). All together capped at `XP_BONUS_CAP` (30%).
- `Progress.buffs()` and `Progress.xpBonus`; `applyModifiers` puts the buffs on the fighter (source `buff`), `addXp` adds the XP bonus (`raw` to skip it). Shown on the Me screen (Buffs), the jeweller's card and the jewellery box, the item details (tier), and GraftWiki's Clothes and jewellery. `tests/gearBonus.test.ts`.

**The barber.** `src/barber/barber.ts`: NeXo Cutz on the High Street (frontage x 68–74, door at x 71, `barber_shop` in `world/interiors.ts`), Vinny's chair (station `barber_chair`).

Props `barber_chair`, `barber_station`, `barber_pole` are code-built (`LevelView`; rows in the asset sheet). GraftWiki's The barber, `barber` hint. `tests/barber.test.ts`.

**The tattoo studio.** `src/tattoo/tattoo.ts`: Ink & Iron on Hollins Estate (frontage x 46–53 by the garages, door at x 50.6, `tattoo_studio` in `world/interiors.ts`), Shaz's bed (station `tattoo_chair`).
- Six places (`SLOT_ORDER`, the appearance's `TATTOO_SLOTS`), 11 designs (`DESIGNS`, `TATTOO_DESIGNS`; the sleeve on upper arms only); price by design and place (`designPrice`, `SLOT_MULT`); laser `LASER_PRICE`, re-ink `REINK_PRICE` a piece. `quote` charges only what changes (an old piece is lasered first); `getInk` pays pocket then tin and writes `save.character.appearance`.
- `ui/tattooUI.ts` on the studio set (`Game.openTattoo`): top off, turned to the place (`TATTOO_YAW`), tabs per place and Ink. Props `tattoo_bed`, `flash_wall` are code-built (asset sheet rows).
- The ink is real on MakeHuman bodies: `render/human/tattooSkin.ts` paints each design onto a copy of the skin texture at its place on the shared UV layout (`SLOT_UV`), multiplied, cached by skin, ink and designs (16 kept). Full bodies only: background people keep the pack's tattooed skin for a heavily inked look. Designs are drawn by `drawDesign` in `render/skinPainter.ts`. `chooseSkin` now keeps baked-tattoo skins (`m_tattooed`, `m_viking`) off people who aren't inked.
- Station NPCs can carry their own ink (`NpcDef.tattoos`; drawn on the full body). GraftWiki's Tattoos, `tattoo` hint, cab stop.

**Cashpoints.** `src/money/atm.ts`: four machines in the whole town (`ATM_SITES`: High Street and Kingsway are Califax, City Centre and Riverside Centre Lloyd's THC, by the prop's `label`), set into shop walls (z -2.05, not solid, a granite surround and the bank's header in `LevelView`). Out of service until Sully's beaten (`ATM_FLAG`). Both banks reach the same account. Pay in free; take out in £10 notes up to `ATM_MAX`, free in town (`FREE`), `PAY_FEE` elsewhere. Withdrawals and deposits go on the statement (finance kind `cash`). `ui/atmUI.ts` is the machine's software: card and auto-PIN sequence, side-button menus, keypad for other amounts (number keys too), balance, mini statement, receipt, branded goodbye; the money moves once, before the counting screens, and nothing can be pressed while they play. Beeps: `AudioManager.atm`. The approved Califax and Lloyd's THC logos aren't in the repo yet: drop them in `public/art/banks/` and set `logo` in `BANKS`. `tests/streetLife.test.ts`.

**Street dealers.** `src/business/streetDealers.ts`: once you're dealing, rough districts (`DEALER_DISTRICTS`) have a rival on a stretch of `DEALER_TRIPS` trips (`rivalIn`, `dealerIn`, fixed by district and stretch). He stands on the street (`World.setUpDealer`, purple £ badge, `dealer` mark); USE (`kind: 'dealer'`, `dealer:talk`) opens `streetDealerScreen` in `ui/dealingUI.ts`: buy at trade (`TRADE_SHARE`, `TRADE_MAX` a stretch), tax him (`taxOffer`: pays a cut or fights, once a stretch), or run him off (`World.runOffDealer`: he and his mates square up). Put him down and his stash and cash drop and the corner is yours for `CORNER_TRIPS` (`takeCorner`, `dealer:down`). Your price there: `competition` (`RIVAL_CUT` with him about, `CORNER_BONUS` once it's yours) into `streetPrice`. Saved in `save.dealing.corners` (tidied when first used). `dealer` hint; GraftWiki's Dealing. `tests/streetLife.test.ts` covers all three.

**Dealing.** `src/business/dealing.ts`: opens after the Ratcatcher (`DEALING_FLAG`), when Kez gets in touch (`Game.dealingNews`).
- Kez sells bulk weed, cocaine and pills (`DRUG_DEFS`, `buyBulk`; his contact screen's Wholesale section, `ui/dealingUI.ts` `wholesaleRows`) and buys it back at 70%. Bulk and deals are `contraband` items, always given as stolen, so the police take them and the pawn shop won't.
- The prep table on the garage floor (`PREP` in `property/home.ts`, station `prep`, only once it's open; `TABLES`: table £1,500, digital scales faster, a second table for two batches): bulk goes on (`loadBulk`), the grow's stock comes up from the basement as bulk weed (`fromGrow`, 10 units a bag), and it bags up on the play clock a batch at a time, a bag per deal (`tickPrep`, `buyBags`); deals wait on the table (`takeDeals`).
- On the street, about 30% of passers-by are punters while you carry deals (`World.punters`, green £ badge; fixed per person and trip): USE sells them 1–3 (`findPunter`, `sellTo`) at `streetPrice` (more for coke further east, less the more you've sold in that district this trip). Every sale is police heat (`SALE_HEAT`, `addHeatOf`) and a `dealing` record; some punters are plain clothes (`undercoverChance`): police arrive. `deal:sold` event; 08 Dealing tutorial guide; GraftWiki's Dealing. `tests/dealing.test.ts`.

**The Arches fight club.** `src/fightclub/club.ts`: the cellar under Kane's Boxing, Canal Side Works (`fight_club`, door at x 84), opens when Sully's beaten (`CLUB_FLAG`); Sully runs the card (station `fight_club`, `ui/fightClubUI.ts`).
- The ladder (`LADDER`, six fighters): buy-in (`BUY_IN`, 20%) in, purse out, you take their place; one fight a night, and a night is `NIGHT_SECONDS` (600 s) of play time (`nightOf`), not a trip, so walking out and in doesn't bring the next fight round.
- The title (`CHAMPION`, Iron Mick Doherty, £3,000): the first win gives `title_belt` (legendary waist slot, unsellable, the only one). Defences every `DEFENCE_TRIPS` nights (`challenger`, harder each time and scaled to your level; `defencePurse`); lose one and the challenger holds it till a rematch (`REMATCH_PURSE`); keep a challenger waiting `STRIP_TRIPS` nights and you're stripped (`clubTick`).
- `World`: `setUpClub` (the crowd and tonight's opponent at `PIT_WAIT`), `startClubFight` (a duel of kind `club`: to the stoppage in the pit, `PIT` in `world/interiors.ts`, no weapons, crew watch; going down loses the fight, never the game), `clubXp`. `Game.clubFight` / `duelEnded` pay out (`settleBout`); boss music while it's on. GraftWiki's The Arches, `club` hint. `tests/fightClub.test.ts`.

**Getting about.** Maps (and the phone's Maps) offer, per place: Directions, **Run there**, **Drive** (with a car) and a Duber cab.
- Run there (`Game.startRun` / `autoCommand`): the fighter runs the route (`nextExit`) with the normal run, through edges and doors; any input takes over; a fight hands control back and the run carries on after it. `travel/onFoot.ts`: each street crossed rolls once to be jumped (`ambushChance`: rough districts, gang heat; none while learning or indoors), `World.ambush` spawns the lads. The card shows the risk (`journeyRisk`).
- Drive (`driveOption`, `Game.driveTo`): free, no fights, from wherever the car is: on the drive, or `save.property.carOut` (where you left it). It's parked on clear road at the other end (`property/parking.ts`), drawn with its paint and mods, with a map pin; one place at a time.
- Car mods (`property/carMods.ts`, Arnold Shark's Mods tab): paint, finish, alloys, tint, spoiler, body kit, ride height, callipers, exhaust, plates, decals; looks only; saved on `OwnedVehicle.mods`; drawn by `render/props/carMods.ts` over the kit model. `tests/travel.test.ts`.

**Shop tiers.** `src/items/shopTiers.ts`: JD Streets sells Basic and Branded from the start, Designer after Sully, Luxury and Iconic after Big Dunc; locked pieces show SOON and can still be tried on.

**The lock-up.** `src/world/lockup.ts`: the Estate King's reward lock-up is its own garage at the end of the estate's garage row (a gap in the kit frontage, built in `LevelView.buildLockup`); its up-and-over door swings up and in while you open it and stays up.

**Shop names.** The brands are display names only (signs, prompts, screens, map, wiki): internal ids, districts and save keys are unchanged. Shop interiors still pick their layout from the sign, so new names are in `interiorFor` / `layoutFor` (`city/street.ts`). The new names have no painted title graphics yet (they fall back to text).

**Heat.** `src/progress/heat.ts`: police, locals and gang heat per district, what raises it (`HEAT`), how fast it cools, and the thresholds. Raise it with `Progress.addHeatOf`, never by writing `save.heat`.

**Loading and performance** (`src/performance/`, PR #98).
- `requests.ts`: every asset download goes through one limit (6) with priorities (`now`, `soon`, `later`, `background`) and `bump`. Download with `assetFetch.ts` (`fetchBytes`, `loadGlb`, `loadTextureFile`, `fetchJson`; 30 s timeout), never `fetch` or a three.js loader directly.
- `levelAssets.ts`: what each location needs from the model sets (`modelSet.ts`, registered in `SETS`). The lists are made by `npx tsx tools/assets/levelDeps.ts` into `levelAssets.generated.ts`. Rerun it after changing what a level draws: `tests/levelAssets.test.ts` fails if they're stale. Home, the basement and the estate are recorded at arrival (`recordLevel`). Play starts once what every location uses is in; `Game.enterArea` holds behind the loading bar until the area's models are in, and `prepareAround` loads the doors' and neighbours' models next.
- `scheduler.ts`: one per-frame budget for background work (`addJob`; districts next door build there through `LevelView.buildUntil`). Don't start your own rAF loops for building.
- People are posed by tier (`FighterView.update`): off camera means not posed at all.
- `LevelView.mergeStatic` merges boxes and cylinders by parent and material once a level's built. Keep anything you'll change later in a LevelView field, and it's left alone.
- Resolution drops only when frames are GPU-bound (`GameRenderer.adaptResolution`). The HUD writes through `ui/dirty.ts`.
- No profiler, FPS counter or debug overlay in the game (the owner's rule). Measure from scripts, e.g. `renderer.info` on a `?debug` page.

**Economy.** `docs/ECONOMY.md` has the targets and where every number lives. Run `npx tsx tools/economy/model.ts` after changing a price or reward; `tests/economy.test.ts` fails if the curve breaks.
