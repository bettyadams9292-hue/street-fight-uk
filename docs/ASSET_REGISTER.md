## Characters (3)

Fighters, bosses and shopkeepers use a skinned model made in Blender (assets-src/sfuk_characters.blend, exported to public/models/characters.glb). Its skeleton has the game's 11 joints at the same positions, so the existing pose animation drives it. The face, hair and headwear are still procedural, on the head bone. The procedural rig remains as a fallback (?classic).
